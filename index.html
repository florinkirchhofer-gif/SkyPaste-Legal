1Controller, scope and summary

App: SkyPaste · Controller: [Your Name or Company Name, full postal address] · Privacy contact: support@skypasteapp.com · Version: 1.0, effective November 2026

SkyPaste (“we”, “us”) is responsible for the personal data described in this Policy. It applies to our mobile, desktop and cross-platform applications and to the support website (together, the “App”).

The App synchronizes the most recent clipboard text between your own devices. We designed it to know as little about you as possible.

In short:

  • We collect your email address, a random user ID, your subscription status, an encrypted clipboard item and limited technical data needed to run and secure the service.
  • Clipboard text is encrypted on your device before it is sent. We store only ciphertext and only the single most recent item per account.
  • We do not sell or share your data, we do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not build profiles.
  • You can have your account and all associated data deleted at any time (section 8).
  • No system is perfectly secure. We use the safeguards in section 6 and tell you what they do and do not protect.

Who this Policy applies to

This Policy applies to everyone who creates an account or uses the App, wherever they live. It does not apply to websites, apps or services of third parties that you reach from the App or from our support page, such as Apple, Microsoft or GitHub. Please read their privacy notices separately.

Our approach to privacy

We follow privacy by design and by default. That means we decide what data the App truly needs before we build a feature, we turn the most protective settings on without you asking, and we remove data when we no longer need it. The clipboard feature is the clearest example: instead of storing your content and promising to look after it, we built the App so that we cannot read it.

Voluntary provision. Email address and encrypted clipboard data are required to use sync. Without them we cannot provide the service.

How your data moves through the App

  1. Sign-up and sign-in. You enter your email address. Our backend creates a random user ID and sends you a single-use Magic Link. When you open it, the App receives a session token that is stored in your device’s secure storage.
  2. Copying. When you copy text on one device, the App encrypts it locally and uploads the ciphertext. It replaces the previous item for your account.
  3. Syncing. Your other signed-in devices download the ciphertext and decrypt it locally with your key.
  4. Subscribing. You buy Pro through Apple or Microsoft. The store tells the App and our backend whether your subscription is active. We never see your payment details.
  5. Signing out or deleting. Signing out removes the token from that device. Deleting your account removes your records from our systems as described in section 8.

Device permissions

The App needs access to the system clipboard to do its job. It does not request access to contacts, photos, files, location, camera or microphone. On some operating systems, you can restrict clipboard access in the system settings. If you do, sync may stop working.

Organizational measures

  • Need-to-know access. Only people who need access to operate the service can reach production systems. Access is reviewed regularly and removed when a role ends.
  • Strong authentication for administrators. Administrative accounts of our providers use multi-factor authentication.
  • Separation of environments. We do not use real user data for development or testing.
  • Confidentiality. Everyone who may come into contact with personal data is bound by a duty of confidentiality.
  • Secrets management. Keys and credentials for our infrastructure are kept out of source code and rotated when exposure is suspected.
  • Logging and monitoring. We monitor for unusual access patterns and review security events.
  • Backups. Backups are encrypted and access to them is restricted.
  • Provider diligence. We choose providers that offer recognized security certifications and contractual security commitments, and we review them when their terms change.

Limits of what we can promise

Encryption protects content, not everything around it. If someone gains access to your unlocked device, to the key stored on it, or to your email account, they may be able to read your clipboard content or sign in as you. We cannot prevent this by technical means on our side. For that reason we cannot guarantee that the App is free of all vulnerabilities, and we ask you to keep your devices and accounts secure.

7Data breach notification

We maintain an incident response process to detect, contain and assess security incidents. If a personal data breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will:

  1. Notify the competent supervisory authority without undue delay and, where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, within 72 hours of becoming aware of it. Under the Swiss FADP the notification goes to the FDPIC as soon as possible.
  2. Notify affected users without undue delay where the breach is likely to result in a high risk, and in line with US state breach laws, Canada’s PIPEDA, Brazil’s LGPD and other local rules.
  3. Tell you what happened, which data is affected, what we have done and what you can do.

Because clipboard content is encrypted on your device, a breach of our database alone would not expose readable clipboard text. Email addresses and metadata could still be affected.

15Your choices and controls

You decide how much of the App’s functionality you use and what you place in it.

  • Decide what you copy. Only text you copy while the App is running and sync is on is uploaded. Pause sync or sign out before you copy something you do not want to leave your device.
  • Sign out. Signing out removes the session token from that device. Sign out on every device to end all access.
  • Replace or clear your clipboard record. Each new item replaces the previous one. Copying a short, harmless text overwrites the stored item.
  • Update your email address. Contact us if your address changes, and we will update your account after verifying the new address.
  • Delete your account. You can request deletion at any time (section 8).
  • Cancel your subscription. Subscriptions are managed in your Apple or Microsoft account settings. Deleting the App or your account with us does not cancel a subscription automatically.
  • Change system permissions. You can restrict clipboard access in your device settings, which may limit sync.
  • Choose your email provider. Because sign-in works through email, using a provider with strong security and two-factor authentication protects your account.

You are not required to give us any data beyond what the service needs, and we do not make the service worse for you because you exercised a right.

  • Sign-in messages. Magic Links and related verification emails. These are sent when you ask for them.
  • Security and account notices. For example a warning about suspicious sign-in attempts, a notice about a data breach or a change to your account.

Be careful with Magic Links: they work like a password. Do not forward them, and do not open them on a device you do not trust. We will never ask you for your password, your encryption key or the content of your clipboard by email.

17Requests from authorities

We handle requests from courts, police and other authorities as follows:

  1. We check that the request is valid, issued by a competent authority and limited to what is necessary.
  2. We challenge requests that are too broad, unclear or unlawful, and we disclose only the minimum data that the law requires.
  3. We tell the affected user about the request unless the law or the authority forbids it.
  4. We keep an internal record of such requests.

What we can provide is limited by our design. We hold your email address, user ID, subscription status, technical logs and the encrypted clipboard record. We cannot decrypt your clipboard content, and we do not have your key. We do not create backdoors or weaken encryption on request.

If you want to know whether we have received requests, contact us. Where permitted, we publish a summary of requests received in a given year on our support page.
